HomeBase Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

HomeBase is operated by Ryan Berenson (“HomeBase,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the HomeBase app, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at Ryanb1113@icloud.com.

1. What HomeBase does

HomeBase helps you keep track of your home: appliances and belongings, model numbers, warranties, paint colors, maintenance reminders, and recurring entertainment subscriptions. It also includes an AI assistant that answers questions using the information you save.

2. Information we collect

Information you add to the app

  • Home inventory details, such as item names, brands, model and serial numbers, purchase dates, prices, stores, warranty dates, rooms, categories, and notes.
  • Quick facts you save, such as filter sizes, paint colors and codes, and the location of water shutoffs or breaker panels.
  • Maintenance reminders and your history of completing them.
  • Subscription details you enter, such as service names, prices, billing dates, your monthly budget, and viewing hours you estimate.

Information you share with the AI assistant

  • The questions you type.
  • Photos you take or upload, and text files you attach.
  • Parts of your saved home and subscription information that are relevant to your question.

Technical information

  • Device type, operating system version, app version, language, and crash or error reports. We do not use third-party analytics. When you use the AI assistant, our server also receives your IP address, which we use to limit abuse.

What we do not collect

  • We never ask for your streaming service passwords, and HomeBase does not connect to your streaming accounts. Subscription and usage information is entered by you and labeled as estimated.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment card numbers.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: store your inventory, send reminders, and calculate budgets and costs.
  • To power the AI assistant so it can answer questions about your home and subscriptions.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse, and fix bugs.
  • To improve the app and develop new features.
  • To respond to support requests and tell you about important changes to this policy.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. The AI assistant

When you ask the assistant a question, we send your message, any photo or file you attach, and the saved information needed to answer it to an AI service provider, Anthropic (the maker of Claude), which generates the response. Anthropic’s commercial terms say that content sent through its API is not used to train its models and is kept only for a limited period. For details, see Anthropic’s current privacy policy and commercial terms. Our server passes your request along and does not save the contents of your questions or photos.

AI answers can be wrong. Color matches from photos are estimates and are affected by lighting. Do not rely on the assistant for safety-critical decisions, such as electrical, gas, or structural work. Consult a qualified professional.

Please avoid sending passwords, government ID numbers, or financial account numbers to the assistant.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the app, such as the AI and hosting providers named below. They may use your information only to provide services to us. Our current providers are Anthropic, which powers the AI assistant, and Render, which hosts the server that connects the app to it.
  • Legal and safety reasons. When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.
  • Business changes. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred, and we will tell you if it affects your privacy choices.

6. Accounts and sharing

HomeBase does not currently have accounts or household sharing, and we do not ask you to sign in. Your information stays on your device. If we add these features, we will update this policy and tell you before they take effect.

7. Permissions on your device

HomeBase may ask for access to the following. Each is optional, and you can change them in your device settings.

  • Camera and photo library: to add item photos and ask the AI about what you photograph.
  • Notifications: to send maintenance, warranty, and billing reminders.

If you decline a permission, the rest of the app still works.

8. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit. Your inventory, reminders, and subscription details are stored on your device. When you use the AI assistant, your request travels over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Anthropic. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

HomeBase stores your information on your device, so you control how long it is kept. Deleting an item, or deleting the app, removes that information from your device. Your information may also be included in your device backups, such as iCloud, according to your device settings. We do not keep a copy of your inventory, reminders, or subscription details on our servers, and our server does not save the contents of assistant requests.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Access and edit your information in the app.
  • Delete individual items, facts, reminders, and subscriptions at any time.
  • Contact us at Ryanb1113@icloud.com with any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask us to correct or delete your information, object to or limit certain uses, and withdraw consent, and, in some places, to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have rights to know, delete, and correct their information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; we do not sell or share it for cross-context advertising. To make a request, email Ryanb1113@icloud.com. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Children

HomeBase is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us, and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

We and our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use safeguards to protect information transferred across borders.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The “Last updated” date above shows when this policy was most recently revised.
